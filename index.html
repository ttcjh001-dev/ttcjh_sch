校園景觀與特色 Campus Features & Landscapes

平頂山腳的活力操場 The vibrant playground at the foot of Pingdingshan Mountain

學校擁有被青翠山林環抱的廣闊運動空間。紅綠相間的操場旁矗立著茂密的大榕樹，讓學生在運動流汗之餘，也能在綠意盎然的樹蔭下乘涼休憩，展現充滿活力的校園風貌。 The school features a spacious sports area surrounded by lush, green mountains. Next to the red and green running track stand towering banyan trees, providing students with a cool and refreshing shaded spot to rest after breaking a sweat, showcasing a vibrant campus atmosphere.

書香盛的生態庭園 An Eco-Garden with a Strong Literary Atmosphere

校園內設有靜謐的圓形生態水池，中央聳立著雅致的假山石景，池畔點綴著古樸的陶甕。在洗石子走廊旁的綠意庭園中，提供學子一個沉澱心靈、修德勵學的絕佳環境。 The campus features a tranquil circular ecological pond with an elegant rockery standing in the center, and is adorned with rustic ceramic pots along the banks. Nestled within the verdant courtyard beside the exposed-aggregate corridor, it provides an ideal sanctuary for students to calm their minds and cultivate their character.

Fun心遊的藝術校園 Joyful Art Exploration Campus

校園建築的每個角落都充滿巧思！穿堂柱面上有著童趣的「愛麗絲夢遊仙境」撲克牌彩繪，操場旁更有著生動的撐傘紳士及3D藝術壁畫，為日常學習生活注入滿滿的創意與想像力。 Every corner of the school campus is filled with clever design. The hallway pillars feature playful, childlike paintings of "Alice in Wonderland" playing cards. Next to the sports field, there are vivid murals of an umbrella-holding gentleman and 3D artwork. These creative touches bring boundless creativity and imagination into daily student life.
