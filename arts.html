🟢 七年級：【鄉土與品德扎根】描繪山水，採集聲景 Grade 7: Painting Landscapes, Collecting Soundscapes

上學期（視覺藝術）：平頂山腳下的寫生 (Painting at the Foot of Pingdingshan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：認識林口台地地貌。學習風景寫生基礎構圖、色彩學與水彩素描技法。Understand Linkou Plateau's landforms. Learn basic composition, color theory, and sketching techniques.
  • 實作任務【泰山綠意寫生】(Task: Greenery Painting)：在校園中眺望平頂山或面對操場茂密榕樹進行實地寫生，記錄家鄉之美。Sketch outdoors overlooking Pingdingshan or the banyan trees to record the hometown's beauty.

下學期（音樂/表演）：大窼溪的自然交響樂 (Nature Symphony of Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：介紹環境音樂與聲景採集。探討溪水潺潺與大自然白噪音帶來的寧靜氛圍。Introduce ambient music and soundscape collection. Explore the tranquil atmosphere of babbling brooks.
  • 實作任務【自然聲景打擊樂】(Task: Soundscape Percussion)：結合老樹鳥鳴，利用身體打擊或環保物創作模仿水流、鳥鳴的節奏。Combine birdsongs and use body percussion or eco-materials to create rhythms mimicking water and birds.

🟡 八年級：【多元整能發展】藝術校園，創藝玩家 Grade 8: Art Campus, Creative Players

上學期（視覺/表演）：走入壁畫的奇幻世界 (Entering the Fantasy World of Murals)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：介紹錯覺藝術、公共藝術及角色扮演，結合校園「愛麗絲」彩繪及「撐傘紳士」壁畫。Introduce illusion art, public art, and role-playing, combining "Alice" and "Umbrella Gentleman" murals.
  • 實作任務【畫中人物活起來了】(Task: Characters Come Alive)：為畫中人物編寫在泰山國中遊玩的短劇，並製作道具實地演出。Write and perform a short play about mural characters playing in the school, using handmade props.

下學期（視覺/音樂）：老樹下的環境劇場 (Environmental Theatre under Old Trees)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合生態水池與老樹，介紹環境劇場與大地藝術。Combine the eco-pool and old trees to introduce environmental theatre and land art.
  • 實作任務【落葉與微風的藝術共創】(Task: Art Co-creation)：撿拾落葉進行曼陀羅地景拼貼，並伴隨樂器演奏，完成戶外發表。Collect fallen leaves for Mandala land art collages, accompanied by musical instruments outdoors.

🔴 九年級：【國際視野升華】登高望遠，世界藝術交匯 Grade 9: World Art Intersection

上學期（視覺藝術）：世界名畫的泰山演繹 (Taishan Interpretation of Masterpieces)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：賞析東西方經典藝術流派，探討藝術家如何展現當時社會與全球文化。Appreciate classic Eastern/Western art movements. Explore how artists present global culture.
  • 實作任務【曉天下・名畫翻玩】(Task: Masterpiece Remix)：選擇世界名畫進行「在地化」二次創作（如將《星夜》換成平頂山背景）。Remix world masterpieces with local elements (e.g., replacing "Starry Night" background with Pingdingshan).

下學期（綜合藝文）：給世界的發聲 (Voice to the World)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：介紹藝術作為表達社會關懷、SDGs 與國際議題的媒介。Introduce art as a medium for expressing social concerns, SDGs, and international issues.
  • 實作任務【畢業藝術策展】(Task: Graduation Art Exhibition)：針對氣候變遷或和平等議題策劃微型展覽，向天下發聲。Curate a micro-exhibition on issues like climate change or peace to voice opinions to the world.
💡 課程亮點：充分利用環境優勢！七年級記錄自然；八年級實踐壁畫；九年級翻玩名畫，培養國際視野！
Course Highlights: Use the environment! Gr.7 records nature; Gr.8 acts out murals; Gr.9 remixes masterpieces for global vision!
