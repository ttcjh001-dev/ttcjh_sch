🟢 七年級：【鄉土與品德扎根】丈量土地，數據尋根 Grade 7: Measuring the Land, Finding Roots through Data

上學期：平頂山的幾何密碼 (Geometric Codes of Pingdingshan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合比例尺、線型函數與直角坐標系。引導學生認識林口台地地形特徵。Combine scale, linear functions, and Cartesian coordinates to understand Linkou Plateau's features.
  • 實作任務【泰山微型地圖製作】(Task: Micro Map Making)：繪製從學校看平頂山的縮放草圖。透過測量與計算，從「丈量」中培養空間感知。Draw scaled sketches of Pingdingshan. Through measurement, cultivate spatial perception of the homeland.

下學期：大窼溪的數據脈動 (Data Pulses of Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合統計圖表與平均數。探討大窼溪的水文變化或生態調查。Combine statistical charts and averages to explore hydrological changes or ecological surveys of Dake Creek.
  • 實作任務【溪水潺潺數據報】(Task: Creek Data Report)：收集大窼溪水質等數據，繪製成統計圖表，反思環境保育重要性。Collect water quality data, draw statistical charts, and reflect on the importance of environmental conservation.

🟡 八年級：【多元整能發展】幾何校園，生活數感 Grade 8: Geometric Campus, Number Sense in Life

上學期：彩繪牆上的黃金比例 (Golden Ratio on Painted Walls)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合對稱、幾何圖形與黃金比例。帶領學生觀察校園內3D立體壁畫與「愛麗絲」彩繪。Combine symmetry, geometry, and golden ratio. Observe 3D murals and "Alice" paintings on campus.
  • 實作任務【Fun心遊・3D錯覺藝術家】(Task: 3D Illusion Artist)：解密3D壁畫的透視原理，設計一幅具有立體錯覺的幾何圖形。Decode the perspective principles of 3D murals and design a geometric figure with a 3D illusion.

下學期：老榕樹與生態池的數理之美 (Math Beauty of Old Trees & Pools)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合數列與圓的性質。在生態水池旁與老樹下進行戶外數學課。Combine sequences and circle properties. Conduct outdoor math classes by the ecological pool and old trees.
  • 實作任務【大自然的數學家】(Task: Nature's Mathematician)：運用「相似三角形」測量老榕樹高度；觀察葉片排列，尋找「費氏數列」。Use "similar triangles" to measure tree height; observe leaf arrangements to find the "Fibonacci sequence".

🔴 九年級：【國際視野升華】數據識讀，放眼天下 Grade 9: Data Literacy, Looking at the World

上學期：勇登泰山，數據看天下 (Scaling Taishan, Seeing the World via Data)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合進階統計與機率。解讀國際新聞、SDGs 或全球氣候變遷數據。Combine advanced statistics and probability. Interpret global news, SDGs, or climate change data.
  • 實作任務【統計陷阱大破解】(Task: Cracking Stat Traps)：判斷國際報導中是否有「被誤導的座標軸」或「機率謬誤」，訓練邏輯思辨。Identify "misleading axes" or "probability fallacies" in international reports to train logical thinking.

下學期：我的世界啟航計畫 (My World Departure Plan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合匯率換算、複利計算與預算規劃等生活金融數學。Combine exchange rates, compound interest, and budget planning in financial mathematics.
  • 實作任務【看見世界的預算書】(Task: World Budget Plan)：規劃未來的國際見學之旅，運用試算表進行跨國匯率換算與預算編列。Plan a future international study trip, using spreadsheets for currency conversion and budgeting.
💡 課程亮點：七年級將數學當作測量工具；八年級解析校園藝術；九年級化為探索世界的羅盤！
Course Highlights: Gr.7 uses math as a measuring tool; Gr.8 analyzes campus art; Gr.9 uses it as a compass to explore the world!
