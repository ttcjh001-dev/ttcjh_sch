孕育泰山的生命之河 The River of Life Nurturing Taishan

大窼溪是流經泰山區的重要水文，自古以來便與在地居民的生活息息相關。清澈的溪水不僅滋養了這片土地，豐富了沿岸的自然生態，更是見證泰山從早期農業社會發展至今的歷史軌跡。

Dake Creek is an important waterway flowing through Taishan District, closely related to the lives of local residents since ancient times. The clear stream not only nourishes this land and enriches the natural ecology along its banks, but also witnesses the historical trajectory of Taishan's development from an early agricultural society to the present.

在泰山國中的校本課程中，大窼溪是一本活生生的自然與社會教科書。我們帶領學生走出教室，認識這條家鄉的生命之河，了解水資源保育的重要性，並從中培養愛鄉愛土的情懷。

In Taishan Junior High School's school-based curriculum, Dake Creek is a living textbook of nature and society. We lead students out of the classroom to get to know this river of life in our hometown, understand the importance of water resource conservation, and cultivate a love for our homeland.

溪水潺潺伴讀書的寧靜氛圍 The Tranquil Atmosphere of a Babbling Brook Accompanying Study

依傍著大窼溪與平頂山，泰山國中擁有得天獨厚的寧靜學習環境。不遠處溪水流動的潺潺聲，彷彿是大自然演奏的白噪音，能夠洗滌喧囂，幫助學生靜下心來專注學習。

Situated beside Dake Creek and Pingdingshan, Taishan Junior High School enjoys a uniquely tranquil learning environment. The babbling sound of the flowing stream not far away is like white noise played by nature, capable of washing away the hustle and bustle, helping students calm down and focus on learning.

漫步在寬敞明亮的校園走廊，或是穿梭在充滿綠意的紅磚步道上，伴隨著微風與水文的氣息，讓「讀書」不再只是枯燥的文字吸收，而是一種與自然共鳴的靈感啟發。

Strolling in the bright and spacious campus corridors, or weaving through the greenery-filled red brick paths, accompanied by the breeze and the breath of the water, "studying" is no longer just dry absorption of text, but an inspiration that resonates with nature.

寧靜的校園林蔭步道，彷彿能感受到大窼溪畔微風拂來的清爽氣息。 The tranquil tree-lined paths on campus make you feel the refreshing breeze from the banks of Dake Creek.
寬敞明亮的走廊，是學子們伴隨自然氣息、靜心閱讀的絕佳學習空間。 The bright and spacious corridors are excellent learning spaces for students to read peacefully while accompanied by the breath of nature.
