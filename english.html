🟢 七年級：【鄉土雙語扎根】用英文說家鄉的故事 Grade 7: Bilingual Local Roots - Telling Hometown Stories in English

上學期：平頂山腳下的我們 (Our Campus at Pingdingshan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：學習與校園環境、自然地理相關的基礎英文詞彙。介紹如何用英文表達「平頂山」的地理特色。Learn basic English vocabulary related to campus environment and natural geography. Explain the features of "Pingdingshan".
  • 實作任務【My Green Campus 導覽小書】(Task: Guidebook)：分組製作簡易英文圖文小書，介紹學校旁綠樹成蔭的操場與生態景觀。Create a simple English picture book introducing the green playground and ecology as a guide for foreign students.

下學期：大窼溪畔的生命之河 (The River of Life: Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合自然生態，學習動植物與水資源相關的英文。認識大窼溪作為孕育泰山人文的生命之河。Learn English related to flora, fauna, and water resources. Understand Dake Creek as the river of life.
  • 實作任務【Water is Life 英文海報設計】(Task: Poster Design)：設計一張全英文的推廣海報，呼籲大眾愛護大窼溪與水資源。Design an all-English promotional poster urging the public to protect Dake Creek and water resources.

🟡 八年級：【多元與品德對話】藝術校園的英語發聲 Grade 8: Diversity & Character - English Voices of the Art Campus

上學期：畫中有話的奇幻校園 (The Magic of Campus Art)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：融入西方童話（如《Alice in Wonderland》）。引導學生用英文描述校園中「愛麗絲」及「撐傘紳士」壁畫。Integrate Western fairy tales. Guide students to describe campus murals like "Alice" and the "Umbrella Gentleman" in English.
  • 實作任務【Campus Art Tour 英語導覽員】(Task: English Tour Guide)：學生扮演英文導覽員，在校園壁畫前錄製一段1分鐘的英文解說影片。Act as an English tour guide and record a 1-minute English explanation video in front of the campus murals.

下學期：會呼吸的校園 (A Breathing Ecological Campus)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：聚焦老榕樹與生態水池。探討人與自然和諧共存的議題，閱讀環境保護的英文短文。Focus on old banyan trees and the ecological pool. Discuss human-nature coexistence and read eco-protection articles.
  • 實作任務【Eco-friendly 廣播劇】(Task: Radio Drama)：分組編寫並錄製一段英文廣播劇，模擬動植物對話，傳達保護生態的品德價值。Write and record an English radio drama simulating animal/plant conversations to convey ecological protection values.

🔴 九年級：【國際視野升華】登高望遠，擁抱世界 Grade 9: International Vision - Scaling Heights, Embracing the World

上學期：勇登泰山，曉天下事 (Global Citizens in Action)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：閱讀國際新聞、探討 SDGs。學習表達個人觀點、同意與反對的高階英文句型。Read international news and discuss SDGs. Learn advanced English patterns for expressing opinions, agreement, and disagreement.
  • 實作任務【TED-style 英文短講】(Task: English Short Talk)：選擇關心的全球性議題，思考身在泰山的我們能做些什麼，並進行全英文簡報。Choose a global issue, reflect on what we can do locally, and give an all-English presentation.

下學期：我的未來藍圖 (My Journey, My Future)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：閱讀名人傳記或勵志演講，探討如何跨越文化障礙，實踐「修德勵學」的精神。Read biographies or motivational speeches, exploring how to overcome cultural barriers and practice virtue/learning.
  • 實作任務【To the World: 給未來的一封信】(Task: Letter to the Future)：用英文寫下一封給未來自己的信（或英文自傳），敘述對未來的期許。Write a letter to your future self in English, detailing your growth and expectations for the international stage.
💡 課程亮點：七年級用英文說泰山故事；八年級活化校園特色；九年級探討世界議題，讓英文成為曉天下的最強工具！
