優雅的藝術氣息：遠近馳名的美術班 Elegant Artistic Atmosphere: The Renowned Art Class

泰山國中是一個充滿藝術氣息的書香校園，有遠近馳名的美術班，在校園多處留下美麗的畫作，妝點優雅的藝術氣息。漫步在校園中，您會驚喜地發現在走廊、穿堂與階梯旁，都留下了充滿想像力的壁畫，為全體師生營造出優美且充滿活力的學習環境。

Taishan Junior High School is a scholarly campus full of an artistic atmosphere. It has a renowned art class that has left beautiful paintings in many places, decorating the campus with an elegant vibe. Strolling through the campus, you will be pleasantly surprised to find imaginative murals left in corridors, hallways, and next to stairs, creating a beautiful and energetic learning environment for all teachers and students.

穿堂磚牆上生動的「愛麗絲夢遊仙境」彩繪，展現學生的豐富創意。 The vivid "Alice in Wonderland" painting on the hallway brick wall shows the rich creativity of the students.
操場階梯旁的「撐傘紳士」3D藝術壁畫，為校園妝點優雅的氣息。 The "Gentleman with an Umbrella" 3D art mural next to the playground stairs adds an elegant touch to the campus.

會呼吸的生態校園：鳥鳴伴隨讀書聲 A Breathing Ecological Campus: Birdsong Accompanying Study

泰山國中也是生態校園，校園處處都有樹齡超過50年的老樹，樹冠非常龐大，綠樹成蔭，處處可見鳥類、松鼠的踪跡，優美的鳥鳴聲伴隨讀書聲，更添泰山國中美麗的景緻。此外，校園內靜謐的生態水池更孕育了豐富的動植物生態，這座會呼吸的校園，就是學生們最棒的大自然教室。

Taishan Junior High School is also an ecological campus. There are old trees over 50 years old everywhere. Their huge canopies provide dense shade, and traces of birds and squirrels can be seen everywhere. The beautiful birdsong accompanying the sound of studying adds to the beautiful scenery of Taishan Junior High School. In addition, the tranquil ecological pool on campus nurtures a rich ecology of flora and fauna. This breathing campus is the best nature classroom for students.

樹冠龐大、綠樹成蔭的老樹，不時能聽見優美的鳥鳴聲伴隨讀書聲。 With huge canopies and dense shade, these old trees often resonate with beautiful birdsong accompanying the sound of studying.
古樸雅致的生態水池與假山石景，讓校園處處充滿自然的生機。 The quaint and elegant ecological pool and rockery landscape fill the campus with natural vitality.
