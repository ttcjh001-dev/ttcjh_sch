🟢 七年級（生物）：【鄉土與品德扎根】尋幽探秘，守護山水 Grade 7 (Biology): Exploring Secrets, Protecting Landscape

上學期：平頂山的自然寶庫 (Nature's Treasury at Pingdingshan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合生態系與生物多樣性。探討林口台地地形如何孕育專屬動植物群落。Combine ecosystems and biodiversity. Explore how Linkou Plateau nurtures specific flora and fauna.
  • 實作任務【平頂山生態圖鑑】(Task: Eco-Field Guide)：進行田野調查，利用平板紀錄植物與昆蟲，製作專屬電子圖鑑。Conduct field surveys, record plants/insects with tablets, and create a digital eco-field guide.

下學期：大窼溪的生命脈動 (Pulse of Life at Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合水域生態系。探討河川食物鏈及水質對生物圈影響。Combine aquatic ecosystems. Explore river food chains and water quality impacts on the biosphere.
  • 實作任務【大窼溪水質檢測】(Task: Water Quality Testing)：使用水質檢測包分析水文健康，並發表「減塑護溪」環保倡議。Use testing kits to analyze water health and advocate for plastic reduction to protect the creek.

🟡 八年級（理化）：【多元整能發展】校園微觀，科學創客 Grade 8 (Physics/Chem): Campus Microcosm, Science Maker

上學期：畫布上的光影魔法 (Light and Shadow Magic on Canvas)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合理化科光學單元。以3D壁畫為教材，探討光線反射、折射與視覺成像。Combine the optics unit. Use 3D murals to explore light reflection, refraction, and visual imaging.
  • 實作任務【光學錯覺創客箱】(Task: Optical Illusion Box)：運用透鏡面鏡與光源，設計能產生「3D立體錯覺」的科學玩具。Use lenses, mirrors, and light sources to design science toys creating "3D optical illusions".

下學期：會呼吸的校園生態 (A Breathing Campus Ecology)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：探討老榕樹的光合作用，以及生態水池的微氣候調節與物質循環。Explore photosynthesis of old banyan trees, microclimate regulation, and material cycles of the pool.
  • 實作任務【校園微型生態瓶製作】(Task: Terrarium Making)：在玻璃瓶中模擬水池與老樹循環系統，打造自給自足的微型生態瓶。Simulate the pool and tree cycles in glass jars to create self-sustaining micro-terrariums.

🔴 九年級（地科/進階）：【國際視野升華】登高望遠，永續地球 Grade 9 (Earth Sci.): Scaling Heights, Sustainable Earth

上學期：勇登泰山，看見氣候變遷 (Seeing Climate Change)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：結合地球科學。從泰山區微型氣候變化，放大至全球溫室效應與極端氣候。Combine Earth Science. Zoom out from Taishan's microclimate to global greenhouse effects and extreme weather.
  • 實作任務【SDGs 氣候高峰會】(Task: Climate Summit)：扮演不同國家代表探討碳排放，提出符合 SDGs 的減碳承諾。Act as country delegates to discuss carbon emissions and propose SDG-compliant reduction commitments.

下學期：科技與永續的解方 (Tech and Sustainable Solutions)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：探討再生能源原理，以及科學技術如何在經濟發展與環保間取得平衡。Explore renewable energy principles and how technology balances economic development and environmental protection.
  • 實作任務【綠能創客發明展】(Task: Green Energy Invention)：針對能源危機提出永續解方，製作「太陽能小車」等專題。Propose sustainable solutions for the energy crisis, building projects like "solar toy cars".
💡 課程亮點：七年級向外探索；八年級解析光學與循環；九年級接軌全球氣候議題！
Course Highlights: Gr.7 explores outward; Gr.8 analyzes optics and cycles; Gr.9 connects with global climate issues!
