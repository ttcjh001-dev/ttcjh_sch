🟢 七年級：【鄉土與品德扎根】探尋山水，飲水思源 Grade 7: Roots of Homeland & Character - Exploring Landscapes

上學期：平頂尋根 (Tracing Roots at Pingdingshan)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：閱讀與泰山、林口台地相關的地方文獻與散文。介紹泰山古地名「山腳」的由來，探討先民在平頂山腳下開墾的歷史足跡。Read local literature related to Taishan and the Linkou Plateau. Explore the history of pioneers settling at the foot of Pingdingshan.
  • 寫作任務【我的家鄉風景】(Writing Task: Hometown Scenery)：引導學生觀察校園背景連綿的青翠山脈與老榕樹，寫出一篇描寫家鄉地景的記敘文。Guide students to observe the green mountains and old banyan trees, writing a narrative essay about their hometown landscape.

下學期：窠溪聽水 (Listening to Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：閱讀自然文學與書寫河流生態的文章。大窠溪是孕育泰山的生命之河，帶領學生認識在地水文與保育。Read nature literature and articles on river ecology. Introduce Dake Creek as the river of life nurturing Taishan.
  • 寫作任務【給河流的一封信】(Writing Task: Letter to the River)：反思人類活動對自然的影響，並撰寫一封倡議環保的信件，將「品德」融入語文學習。Reflect on human impacts on nature and write an advocacy letter for environmental protection, integrating character education.

🟡 八年級：【多元整能發展】藝術對話，生態書寫 Grade 8: Holistic Development - Artistic Dialogue & Ecological Writing

上學期：校園尋寶與創意思考 (Campus Treasure Hunt & Creative Thinking)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：跨領域結合視覺藝術。在國文課中介紹「童話改寫」與「魔幻寫實」，呼應校內「愛麗絲」與「撐傘紳士」壁畫。Combine visual arts by introducing "fairy tale rewriting" and "magical realism" corresponding to campus murals.
  • 寫作任務【畫中的奇幻旅程】(Writing Task: Fantasy Journey in the Painting)：選擇校園中的一幅壁畫作為起點，發揮想像力進行故事接龍或微小說創作。Choose a mural as a starting point to write a collaborative story or micro-fiction.

下學期：老樹與生態的低語 (Whispers of Old Trees & Ecology)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：走出教室，在老榕樹下或生態水池旁進行「五感體驗」閱讀。選讀描寫動植物的現代詩與散文。Step out of the classroom for a "five senses experience" reading under old banyan trees or by the ecological pool.
  • 寫作任務【校園生態新詩創作】(Writing Task: Eco-Poetry Creation)：將鳥鳴、微風與讀書聲化為詩句。實踐「修德勵學」中沉穩內斂的品德修養。Turn birdsong, breeze, and study sounds into poetry, practicing calm and introverted character building.

🔴 九年級：【國際與視野升華】登高望遠，胸懷世界 Grade 9: International Vision - Scaling Heights, Embracing the World

上學期：勇登泰山，放眼天下 (Scaling Taishan, Looking at the World)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：選讀翻譯文學、國際新聞報導，或是探討全球性議題（如氣候變遷、文化差異）的深度文章。Read translated literature, international news, or in-depth articles on global issues like climate change.
  • 寫作任務【國際議題專題報導】(Writing Task: Global Issue Report)：分組蒐集國際議題，進行資料統整與批判性思考，並以簡報形式發表。Collect global issues in groups, conduct critical thinking, and present them in a briefing format.

下學期：知行合一的成年禮 (A Coming-of-Age Ceremony of Action)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：閱讀人物傳記與勵志文學，探討不同國家偉人「修德勵學」的生命歷程，回顧成長。Read biographies and inspirational literature, exploring the life journeys of great figures worldwide.
  • 寫作任務【給未來自己的一份自傳】(Writing Task: Autobiography to the Future Self)：寫下屬於自己的成長故事與未來期許，完成國中階段的成年禮。Write your own growth story and future expectations, completing the coming-of-age ceremony of junior high.
💡 課程亮點：讓「閱讀與寫作」不再侷限於課本。七年級扎根在地；八年級結合校園特色；九年級打開世界之窗！
Course Highlights: Reading and writing go beyond textbooks. Grade 7 roots locally; Grade 8 integrates campus features; Grade 9 opens the window to the world!
