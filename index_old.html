校園特色與景觀 Campus Features & Landscapes

依傍著平頂山與大窼溪，泰山國中擁有得天獨厚的自然生態環境。
我們結合在地地景與人文歷史，打造獨具特色的校本課程，帶領學生走出教室，在自然中探索，在體驗中學習。

Situated beside Pingdingshan and Dake Creek, Taishan Junior High School boasts a unique natural ecological environment. Combining local landscapes with cultural history, we create distinctive school-based curricula, leading students out of the classroom to explore in nature and learn through experience.
