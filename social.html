🟢 七年級：【鄉土與品德扎根】探尋山腳，飲水思源 Grade 7: Exploring Shanjiao, Remembering the Source

上學期：平頂山下的「山腳」聚落 (The 'Shanjiao' Settlement)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：認識泰山區地理與歷史。探討為何被稱為「平頂山」，追溯古地名「山腳」由來。Learn Taishan's geography/history. Explore the names "Pingdingshan" and "Shanjiao".
  • 實作任務【泰山尋根地繪】(Task: Root Mapping)：分組田野調查，繪製包含平頂山與舊時聚落的人文歷史地圖。Conduct field research and draw cultural/historical maps including Pingdingshan and old settlements.

下學期：大窼溪的農業與變遷 (Agriculture and Changes of Dake Creek)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：探討清澈溪水如何滋養土地、見證泰山農業社會發展軌跡。Explore how the clear stream nourishes the land and witnesses Taishan's agricultural development.
  • 實作任務【水資源倡議行動】(Task: Water Advocacy)：探討水文環境污染與保育，舉辦水資源保護倡議會，實踐愛護環境。Discuss water pollution/conservation and hold advocacy meetings to practice environmental care.

🟡 八年級：【多元整能發展】社區營造與環境公民 Grade 8: Community Building & Environmental Citizenship

上學期：畫中有話的社區營造 (Community Building Through Art)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：從校園3D藝術壁畫出發，延伸至社區營造與地方創生，探討公共藝術形塑身分認同。Start from 3D murals to explore community building, placemaking, and how public art shapes identity.
  • 實作任務【在地文化策展人】(Task: Local Culture Curator)：規劃一條結合校園藝術與周邊社區景點的文化導覽路線。Plan a cultural tour route combining campus art and surrounding community landmarks.

下學期：老樹、生態與環境權 (Old Trees, Ecology, and Environmental Rights)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：以校內老樹及生態池出發，引入公民課程中「環境權」、永續發展與社會責任概念。Use old trees and pools to introduce concepts of "environmental rights", sustainability, and social responsibility.
  • 實作任務【環境守護公聽會】(Task: Public Hearing)：舉辦模擬公聽會，針對地方開發與生態保育衝突進行辯論。Hold mock public hearings to debate conflicts between local development and ecological conservation.

🔴 九年級：【國際視野升華】登高望遠，世界公民 Grade 9: Scaling Heights, Global Citizens

上學期：勇登泰山，放眼全球經貿 (Global Economy and Trade)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：從泰山產業轉型延伸至全球化貿易分工，探討台灣在世界經濟版圖中的角色。Extend from Taishan's industrial transition to global trade division, exploring Taiwan's role in the world economy.
  • 實作任務【國際專題展】(Task: Global Exhibition)：選擇在地產業，追溯其背後「全球供應鏈」，解析在地與全球的連結。Trace the "global supply chain" behind local industries and analyze local-global connections.

下學期：曉天下事的未來公民 (Future Citizens Knowing the World)

  • 課程內容 (Course Content)：探討聯合國 SDGs、國際 NGOs、氣候變遷與人權議題。Discuss UN SDGs, international NGOs, climate change, and human rights issues.
  • 實作任務【世界公民高峰會】(Task: Global Citizen Summit)：辦理模擬聯合國會議，代表不同國家針對全球議題提出解方。Hold a Model UN conference, representing different countries to propose solutions for global issues.
💡 課程亮點：人文史地全面立體化！從探尋鄉土，到環境公民，最終成為世界公民。
Course Highlights: Humanities & History in 3D! From exploring roots, to environmental citizenship, to becoming global citizens.
